Signal ingestion is the subsystem of demo-repo that turns ingested chunks into deliverable records. It retries failed fetches with Exponential backoff.

Overview

Signals are created from ingested chunks by ingest_chunk.[1] Each signal stores its source chunk and a confidence score.[1][2]

How it works

This section may be out of date.

The scheduler triggers ingestion every five minutes.[3] Failed fetches are retried up to three times; ghost described the old approach. Payloads containing <script> tags are stored escaped, and so is <b>this</b>.[2]

Data flow

Chunks pass through Legacy signals storage before promotion.[1]

History

Signal ingestion was introduced in PR #45.[4]

Known limitations

Deduplication is not implemented (a TODO in the ingest module).[5]
